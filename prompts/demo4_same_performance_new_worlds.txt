Same character, same performance, five different worlds. The kitten in the reference IMAGE (cream-and-ginger, large round black eyes, pink dress, pale pink scarf, standing upright like a person) performs one continuous action: it looks around curiously, takes two steps forward, stops, and raises both paws in delight. That exact performance, timing and locked medium camera repeat identically in each world.

The world changes every three seconds with a hard cut, in this order:
1. inside a canvas camping tent, warm lantern light
2. a mossy forest path, dappled sunlight
3. a wooden horse stable, straw on the floor, a horse's head in the background
4. a busy outdoor market with striped awnings and fruit stalls
5. a stone castle hall with tall windows and banners

The kitten's face, fur, outfit and proportions are identical in every world; only the environment and its lighting on the kitten change.

Audio: ambience matching each world (tent canvas, birdsong, stable sounds, market chatter, echoing hall), no music, no dialogue.
